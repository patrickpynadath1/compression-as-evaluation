Construct $d_D=A(D_{\mathrm{fit}})$ and define

$$
\widehat\mu_D=
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^{n}\delta_{r_{C,d_D}(x_i)},
\qquad
\widehat\mu_G=
\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^{m}\delta_{r_{C,d_D}(y_j)}.
$$

Our proposed score is

$$
\boxed{
\widehat{\mathcal D}_{C,d_D}
(D_{\mathrm{test}},G_{\mathrm{test}})
=
W_1(\widehat\mu_D,\widehat\mu_G).
}
$$

**Lower means closer conditional coding-rate distributions.** This is not a guaranteed distance between the original text distributions: different text distributions can induce identical scalar-rate distributions.
